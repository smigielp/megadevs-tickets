#4471 breakdown · two distinct problems

Ticket #4471 merged 412 reports about dates landing on the wrong day. The desk marked it as one issue ("parser reads mm/dd in eu locales"), but analysis shows two distinct problems that require separate fixes.

Two problems found. 379 reports trace to the parser, 33 to the booking-v3 form hint.

The two problems

1. Parser problem (379 reports)

What happens: The shared date parser reads all dates month-first (MM/DD), regardless of tenant locale. Day-first tenants write dates as DD/MM, but the parser flips them.

Impact:

  • 379 reports (92% of #4471)
  • 11 tenants affected
  • Channels: app (204), email (150), form (25)
  • Both booking-v2 and booking-v3
  • First seen 2026-04-01, last seen 2026-10-04

Example: Tenant harbour.dental (en-GB locale) reports "Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July" — the parser read 07/04 as July 4th instead of April 7th.

Root cause: Parser assumes MM/DD/YYYY format for all input, ignoring tenant locale.

Fix: Parser must respect tenant locale and parse dates according to locale date format (day-first vs month-first).

Tracked: smigielp/megadevs-tickets#1

2. Form hint problem (33 reports)

What happens: The booking-v3 form shows "MM/DD/YYYY" as the date field hint for all tenants. Day-first locale users follow this hint and enter dates month-first, but the form reads the input according to the tenant's locale (day-first), causing a flip.

Impact:

  • 33 reports (8% of #4471)
  • 3 tenants affected: alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour
  • Channel: form only
  • booking-v3 only (booking-v2 shows correct DD/MM/YYYY hint)
  • First seen 2026-04-17, last seen 2026-09-22

Example: User at en-GB tenant sees hint "MM/DD/YYYY", enters 04/07/2026 (meaning April 7), but the form interprets it as DD/MM format (7th of April), which then gets flipped by the parser.

Root cause: booking-v3 form displays hardcoded "MM/DD/YYYY" hint regardless of tenant locale.

Fix: booking-v3 form hint must match tenant locale:

  • Day-first locales: show "DD/MM/YYYY"
  • Month-first locales: show "MM/DD/YYYY"

Tracked: smigielp/megadevs-tickets#2

Why this matters

Merging these as one ticket (#4471) makes sense for user-facing communication — both cause the same symptom. But they need separate fixes in different parts of the codebase:

  1. Parser fix: shared date parsing logic (affects all channels)
  2. Form hint fix: booking-v3 form component only

Fixing only the parser would still leave 33 reports unresolved. Fixing both eliminates all 412 reports.

Analysis method

The support desk's own cause determination logic (src/lib/kb/causes.ts) was used to classify each report. Reports were grouped by:

  • Tenant locale (day-first vs month-first)
  • Channel (app, email, form)
  • Form version (booking-v2 vs booking-v3)
  • Date hint shown to user

This revealed that the 58 form-channel reports split into two groups:

  • 25 reports from booking-v2 or day-first+booking-v3 → parser problem
  • 33 reports from day-first+booking-v3 with MM/DD hint → hint problem
